# M-7 Work orders

## M-7.1 What a work order contains

Every work order names the unit, the alert time, the alert level and drivers, the suspected fault with its manual section, the
steps to perform, the parts to bring and the safety steps (M-6.3). A work order created by Wrench is always a draft until a
maintenance planner approves it.

## M-7.2 Priority

An act alert with an air-system driver becomes a priority 2 order: inspect at the next depot stop. An LPS event (M-4.4) becomes
priority 1: inspect before the next run. A watch only becomes a work order when it repeats (M-3.3), and then gets priority 3.
