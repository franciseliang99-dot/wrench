# M-6 Procedures

## M-6.1 Leak test with the train parked

Park the train with all consumers idle. Let the compressor reach the upper setpoint and unload. Record reservoir pressure, then
record it again after 10 minutes without the compressor loading. A drop of more than 0.5 bar in 10 minutes means a leak. Find it
by listening along the pipe runs or with leak-detection spray on fittings, hoses and cylinder seals. Tighten or replace the part,
then repeat the test until the drop is within limits.

## M-6.2 Dryer tower check

With the unit loading, watch DV_pressure through two tower changes. It should return to near zero within a few seconds of each
change. If one tower does not hold pressure, isolate it, replace the purge valve seal (kit `WD-KIT-07`) and repeat the check.
Desiccant should be replaced every 4 000 running hours or when the outlet air is wet.

## M-6.3 Isolation before work

Switch off and lock the compressor supply breaker, and hang a personal lock and tag. Open the reservoir drain until the gauge
reads zero. Wait for hot parts to cool. Remove your lock only after the unit has been reassembled and inspected.

## M-6.4 When to escalate

Escalate to a senior engineer when: LPS was raised (M-4.4); the leak test fails twice after repair; the motor protection tripped
twice; or the symptom does not match any section of this manual. Do not guess a repair that the manual does not describe.
