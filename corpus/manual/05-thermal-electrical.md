# M-5 Thermal and electrical faults

## M-5.1 Oil temperature high

Symptoms: Oil_temperature above its usual range during normal load cycles. Likely causes: low oil level (M-5.2), a blocked oil
cooler, or a unit that loads far more often than usual because of a leak (M-4.1). Action: check the oil level first, then clean
the cooler fins. If the duty cycle is high, treat the leak, not the temperature.

## M-5.2 Oil level low

Symptoms: Oil_level is set, often together with rising Oil_temperature. Action: with the unit stopped and cooled (M-1.3), top up
with the oil grade on the unit's plate and look for oil on the housing, hoses and drain. An oil level that drops again within a
week means an oil leak; open a work order.

## M-5.3 Motor current high

Symptoms: Motor_current above about 9 A outside the start-up peak, or start-up peaks that last longer than usual. Likely causes:
worn bearings, a seized screw element or low supply voltage. Action: stop the unit and inspect the drive. Do not reset a motor
protection trip more than once without an inspection.

## M-5.4 Motor current low under load

Symptoms: Motor_current near the unloaded value while COMP shows the compressor should be loading. Likely causes: an intake valve
that stays closed or a slipping coupling. Action: check the intake valve signal and the coupling (see M-4.3).
