# WD-200 Service Manual (fictional)

This manual describes the WD-200, a **fictional** rotary-screw air compressor written for the Wrench demo.
No real manufacturer, product, person or part number is described here. Part codes such as `WD-KIT-07` are invented.

The sensor channels in chapter M-2 are named after the channels of the public MetroPT-3 dataset
(UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI 10.24432/C5VW3R, CC BY 4.0), so that the alerts Wrench raises on that data
can be explained with this manual. Setpoints and procedures are illustrative, not engineering guidance.

Sections are numbered `M-<chapter>.<section>`. Answers in Wrench cite these numbers.
